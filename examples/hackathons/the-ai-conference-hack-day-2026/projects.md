# Projects built on Crusoe at The AI Conference Hack Day 2026

[The AI Conference Hack Day 2026](https://hackersquad.io/events/the-ai-conference-hack-day-2026) ran on September 29, 2026 at Pier 48 in San Francisco, organized by [HackerSquad](https://hackersquad.io/) alongside The AI Conference. Crusoe sponsored the event and funded the overall cash prize pool; projects had to use Crusoe to qualify for the overall prizes.

Teams submitted 83 projects, and the 40 below list Crusoe in their stack on their HackerSquad project page. Descriptions and the Crusoe usage notes come from each project page and, where the repository is public, its README. Project names link to the HackerSquad project page, which holds the full description, team and recording. 30 of the 40 repositories were public when this list was compiled; the rest are marked as not public. The same data is in [projects.json](projects.json).
